How it's worked out: Travel = Offset × 1.414. Cut piece = Travel − take-off of both 45s. Results round to the nearest 1/16″.

Measuring take-off: lay a 45 on its side and mark the point where the centerlines of its two ends cross. Measure from that point straight along one end's centerline to where the pipe stops: the socket bottom or hub shoulder on glue and solder fittings, the end of thread engagement on threaded fittings. The manufacturer's dimension sheet lists it too, usually as "G" or "center to socket bottom".
